Pancakes for breakfast. Top with butter and syrup.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp white sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter
  • Butter and maple syrup for serving

Steps

  1. Whisk together flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar in a bowl.
  2. In another bowl, mix milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
  4. Heat a lightly greased griddle or pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour 1/4 cup batter per pancake. Flip when bubbles form on the surface.
  6. Cook until golden on both sides. Serve with butter and syrup.
