Spaghetti

Spaghetti with meat sauce. Easy to make at home.

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti noodles
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 jar (680 g) marinara sauce
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated parmesan for serving

Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water according to package directions. Drain.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan. Sauté onion and garlic until soft.
  3. Add ground beef and cook until browned. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Stir in marinara sauce and oregano. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. Toss sauce with spaghetti or serve sauce over noodles.
  6. Top with parmesan and serve.
