Lasagna

Lasagna with meat sauce and cheese. Bake before serving.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 jar (680 g) marinara sauce
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cook lasagna noodles according to package directions. Drain.
  2. Brown ground beef with garlic. Stir in marinara sauce and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Mix ricotta, egg, and half the parmesan in a bowl.
  4. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  5. Layer noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella. Repeat.
  6. Top with remaining cheese. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 35–40 minutes.
  7. Let rest 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
