Fried Chicken

Fried chicken with a crispy coating. Serve with rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • Oil for deep frying

Steps

  1. Soak chicken pieces in buttermilk for at least 1 hour.
  2. Mix flour, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  3. Dredge each piece in the flour mixture until fully coated.
  4. Heat oil to 350°F (175°C) in a deep pan.
  5. Fry chicken in batches for 12–15 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  6. Drain on paper towels and serve warm.
